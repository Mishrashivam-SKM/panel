# 💎 BlueStone – AI-Powered Jewelry Customization (Frontend Prototype)

A modern, AI-inspired jewelry customization platform that reimagines how users discover, design, and purchase fine jewelry.
This project focuses on **personalization, transparency, and trust**, built entirely as a **frontend functional prototype**.

---

## 🚀 Live Concept

Instead of browsing endless catalogs, users **co-create a unique jewelry piece** through an interactive AI-style design experience.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

The traditional jewelry buying experience suffers from:

* Overwhelming product choices (Paradox of Choice)
* Limited access to custom jewelry design
* Lack of transparency and trust in online high-value purchases

---

## ✅ Solution

This prototype demonstrates how technology can:

* Enable **AI-driven co-creation**
* Democratize custom jewelry design
* Build trust through transparent pricing and interaction

---

## 🌟 Key Features

### ✨ Style Your Jewel – AI Design Generator

* Users select:

  * Occasion
  * Style (modern, vintage, minimal)
  * Gemstone preferences
  * Color palette & mood
* A smart frontend algorithm generates a **unique jewelry design in real time**

### 📊 Business Intelligence Dashboard

* Interactive charts for:

  * Revenue distribution
  * CAPEX & OPEX
  * ROI & growth metrics
* Built using **Chart.js**

### 💰 Transparent Pricing Flow

* Dynamic price breakdown
* EMI & festive offer logic
* No hidden costs

### 🎨 Clean & Responsive UI

* Mobile-friendly layout
* Elegant design inspired by premium jewelry brands

---

## 🛠 Tech Stack

**Frontend**

* HTML5
* CSS3 (Flexbox, Grid)
* JavaScript (ES6+)

**Libraries & Tools**

* Chart.js – Data visualization
* Figma – UX & user flow design
* Google Vertex AI (Image Generation – conceptual use)

---

## 🧠 How the “AI” Works (Frontend-Only)

* No backend or ML model is used
* A **procedural JavaScript algorithm**:

  * Combines predefined design components
  * Adapts output based on user input
* Delivers a fast, realistic AI-like experience suitable for prototyping

---

## 📈 Revenue Model (Conceptual)

### Primary Revenue (93%)

* Online jewelry sales
* Offline omnichannel sales

### Secondary Revenue (7%)

* Premium memberships
* AI consultation (B2B)
* Extended warranties
